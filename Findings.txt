SUPPLY CHAIN BUSINESS FINDINGS



1. Order performance
•	Total orders analyzed: 1,500.
•	Delivered orders: 1,236 (82.4%).
•	In Transit: 123 orders.
•	Delayed orders: 88.
•	Cancelled orders: 53.

2. Product category performance
•	Furniture generated the highest total profit: 3,041,930.54.
•	Electronics ranked second with 2,655,688.59.
•	FMCG had the highest order count at 328 but the lowest total profit at 226,689.03.

3. Product performance
•	Bookshelf generated the highest total product profit: 759,658.05.
•	Storage Cabinet ranked second in total profit at 743,897.29.
•	Storage Cabinet had the highest average profit per order at 11,271.17.

4. Regional performance
•	Asia generated the highest total profit: 1,634,210.14.
•	North America had the highest order count at 330.
•	Asia had the highest delayed-order percentage at 6.98%.
•	Africa had the lowest delayed-order percentage at 4.53%.

5. Transportation performance
•	Rail generated the highest total profit at 1,966,012.59.
•	Rail also had the highest average profit per order at 5,242.70.
•	Air had the highest average shipping cost at 42.62.
•	Sea had the lowest average shipping cost at 11.91.

6. Monthly trends
•	September 2024 had the highest monthly order count at 79.
•	April 2024 had the highest monthly total profit at 418,187.13.
•	October 2025 had the highest delayed-order percentage at 9.38%.
•	October 2024 had the lowest delayed-order percentage at 1.69%.

7. Profitability
•	Total profit across all orders: 7,257,202.89.
•	Average profit per order: 4,838.14.
•	There were 57 orders with negative profit.
•	Cancelled orders accounted for 53 of these loss-making orders, with a combined profit of -7,771.04.

8. Business recommendations
•	Review FMCG pricing, costs, and product mix to understand its low profit.
•	Investigate the causes of higher delayed-order percentages in Asia and during October 2025.
•	Review cancelled orders to understand the losses associated with them.
•	Compare transportation costs alongside delivery requirements before selecting a transport mode.
•	Monitor monthly profit and delayed-order percentages to identify changes in performance.

Note:
These findings describe the sample dataset. 
Higher profit does not necessarily mean higher profit margin, and the dataset does not include promised delivery dates for measuring contractual on-time delivery.
